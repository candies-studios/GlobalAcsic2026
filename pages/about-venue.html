About the Venue

The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai

Location & Access

The Taj Mahal Palace overlooks the Arabian Sea in South Mumbai's heart, providing convenient access to major business districts, cultural landmarks, and transport hubs.

World-Class Facilities

The palace combines heritage architecture with modern conference infrastructure, offering:

  • Advanced meeting spaces and ballrooms
  • High-quality audio-visual support
  • Professional event services and hospitality
  • Multiple dining and networking areas
  • Comfortable accommodation for delegates

Strategic Significance

The choice of this iconic hotel reflects the event's commitment to excellence and creates an inspiring environment where global leaders, policymakers, and financial experts can engage in collaborative dialogue.

Cultural Experience

Beyond conference facilities, delegates can explore Mumbai's nearby historic sites, waterfront areas, and local culture, experiencing the city's dynamic, diverse, and globally connected character.
